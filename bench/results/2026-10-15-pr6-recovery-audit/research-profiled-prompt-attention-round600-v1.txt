## Prior evidence and corrections

R564 established CH32, WG128, subgroup16 and 8x16x16 joint-matrix tiles. At 2,051 selected cells, the loop executes up to 65 chunks; useful QK+PV work is 50,405,376 FLOP/query (12 heads per KV head, 256 dimensions), while INT8 K/V plus scale operands are about 2.17 MB/query logical traffic. Its initial 60,880-B accessor claim was corrected by R568: mode-1 accessor declarations are 60,880 B indirect and 44,500 B Direct. Device admission still uses a 60,880-B reported-local-memory threshold. These source numbers are not compiler resource measurements.

R568's split-head CH64 proposal has 53,876 B of declared mode-1 SLM with Direct and 62,064 B indirect (correcting the report's 62,068 typo). At 2,051 cells it reduces serial chunk steps 65→33 but doubles independently staged K/V requests for the two head groups (about 2.10→4.20 MB/query code bytes, plus scales) and launches twice as many work-groups. Two 8-row tiles preserve 16 padded rows across the query/KV pair. Candidate useful FLOP remains 50.405 MFLOP/query; matrix-issued work estimate rises about 1.54% from tail-cell padding. R568 did not measure speed, compiler allocation, launch admission, or occupancy.

The existing `STRATA_SYCL_PROMPT_DIRECT` switch is selected when its first character is `1`. It avoids local `final` output staging and its final copy/barrier. The Oct 4 screen measured synthetic FP16 KV, 512 queries, selected counts 8…4096, on the documented AOT binary: median 70.382455 ms staged vs 48.479874 ms Direct, and all six outputs were bitwise equal. This is a useful Direct-mode feasibility signal only. It was FP16 KV and synthetic data, not the current mode-1 INT8 selected-ID workload, not the current `launch<1,false,false>` profile, and not an end-to-end model result. The standalone Direct-probe artifact remains unbuilt/untested; it supplies no INT8 result.

## At most two actionable candidates

1. **Measure existing Direct mode first on the observed INT8 route.** Use the exact pinned binary/source family and same 34,603-token input, selected IDs/steps, INT8 pages, residency, queue, chunk size and 128-token output; compare Direct off/on with route and mode counters proving `launch<1,false,false>` versus `launch<1,true,false>`. Hold the 8x16x16 hi/lo matrix math and all row mapping unchanged. Source-level savings are removal of the 16×256 FP32 `final` local array (16,384 B/work-group), replacing its final-stage stores/load/copy with direct normalized output stores and removing the final barrier; the output remains exact-expression FP32 normalization. Since current profile already reports 4,096 B static SLM, do not interpret that table entry as the complete SLM delta or claim an occupancy increase. The direct route's declared accessor sum falls from 60,880 to 44,500 B, but the current host admission threshold remains 60,880 B and a selected route only proves it passed that guard. This candidate adds no arithmetic and should preserve the same online-softmax order, K/V integer codes, four-group scales, hi/lo splits and FP32 accumulation. Exact equivalence is expected by source intent but must be checked; compiler scheduling and direct-store indexing still create correctness risks. Main should compare attention output with its stated tolerance, first/final logits, exact greedy IDs, finite values, guards, missing rows and zero-width rows. Require multiple clean paired samples and fault checks. Do not claim the synthetic FP16 direct screen qualifies mode-1 performance.

2. **If Direct still leaves a large matrix cost, test a mode-1 CH64 geometry behind an opt-in path.** Prefer a single-WG reusable-K/V geometry if the existing 8x16x16 mapping can support it; otherwise R568's concrete 2×WG64 head split is the source-reviewed bounded fallback design, with live-head tiles 8+4 or 6+6, CH64 and Direct output. It targets fewer loop trips (65→33 at 2,051 cells), while retaining both hi/lo matrix terms, FP32 accumulators, scale order, online normalization and exact selected-row set. For the split design, declare and recompute resources from source: 53,876 B Direct accessors/group; duplicated K/V loads and row mapping; extra work-group count. Establish device local-memory admission and compiler-reported allocation/spill/register behavior before full model run. A single-WG reuse design would need exact subgroup ownership and barrier proof before it is preferable. Compare against current CH32 on widths around 31/32/33, 63/64/65, 2,051 and near 256K, with arbitrary sparse/reordered selected IDs, page boundaries, negative/unmapped IDs, counts clipped to cap, partial chunks, zero width, and full 256K tails. The arithmetic itself can remain exact in algorithm and precision, though changed reduction/online-softmax order may produce floating-point differences; this is not an approximate-attention algorithm. Require attention/logit tolerances, IDs, NaN/Inf and guard checks, long-context retrieval/quality checks, and full 256K validation before adoption.

Do not implement a top-k/sparse approximation, drop the hi/lo low component, change exponent or scaling order, or narrow selected-ID coverage: arbitrary selected IDs and tail semantics are the kernel contract. In both candidates, prove empty rows remain zero, invalid/missing page rows are excluded from softmax rather than treated as valid zero-score tokens, and no uninitialized staged cell is read. Direct stores must cover each of the 24 query heads exactly once. For CH64 split groups, distinct head ownership must avoid overlap and omit no head; all group barriers remain converged. Denormal/NaN handling and finite behavior should be compared with current kernel explicitly.

## Decision and limits

Prioritize the existing Direct on/off INT8 comparison because it is selectable today, removes known output staging, and avoids changing matrix work geometry. Keep CH64 as the second gated experiment. Neither is measured on INT8 selected prompt attention yet; only the base CH32 route is proven active in the 34K profile. The 44.066-s entry makes this a material target in that complete diagnostic but supplies no exclusive prefill wall share, no clean throughput claim, and no evidence of gain. CUDA FA2 results and other projects' implementations inform tiling, subgroup cooperation and backend-shape discipline only; they do not establish Arc B570 performance. Main's CPU-repeat analysis remains separate.
