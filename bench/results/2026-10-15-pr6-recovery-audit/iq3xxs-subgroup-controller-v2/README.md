The separately bounded debugger envelope adds at most256MiB; it is recorded before launch, not an unbounded exception. No raw cleanup occurs.

KernelProgressProbe subclasses StackProbe only to observe UR stderr and transport stdout. Stdout bytes do not reset quiet because fixture case output may be buffered. Starting at offset0, every50ms it reads64KiB chunks up to4MiB per scan, retaining at most16KiB of a partial line; complete lines also have16KiB bounds. Successful complete urKernelCreate or urEnqueueKernelLaunch/WithArgsExp return records alone reset quiet. NOT_READY, warnings, event polling and arbitrary stdout do not count. Every byte is scanned once; offset and local line sequence increase, and count/last sequence/last line/scan bytes are bounded compact evidence. UR does not expose the native phase seq field, so local sequence is explicitly a collector line ordinal. A trace-format/budget failure aborts incomplete, not a manufactured progress success.

Independent of progress, latest trigger is native wall minus65seconds (175 quick/835 full); attach is refused at wall minus60seconds or later, preserving the requested60-second minimum reserve. Original owner polling occurs before capture. After a successful first capture, controller continues only transport/original-owner supervision until natural exit or the original240/900-second timeout. It never launches another arm. Even native exit0 after capture is recorded with passed=false/complete=false; capture is diagnostic evidence, never diagnostic PASS or performance PASS. Failed capture also consumes the attempt and prevents further work.
