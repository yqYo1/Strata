## Review findings

**R1013 — accept with scope caveats.** The report answers the pinned workgroup/SLM/barrier question with source-specific accounting, compares the existing row-major, subgroup-softmax and tile-packed paths, and gives useful counterevidence and measurement gates. Its arithmetic agrees with the pinned implementation: the optimized dispatch selects `prefix_batch_impl<33,false,false>` only when that selector is configured; declared local payload is 28,224 bytes; explicit workgroup barriers are one before the loop plus five per cell tile. The QK/PV joint-matrix operation counts are shape/API counts, not evidence of device issue rate or utilization, as the report correctly says.

The closest indexed material is R568 (head split/SLM, SHA `5201cc7899477d1f57793678f4033bc23180aedd94d5f0f2badab9ce3cb02358`), R605 (mapping correction, SHA `a86280b15503eedcac57fb52ae08a93d596a52e8875e0f191d8fbc10c6487be2`), and the R954/R955 subgroup review (report SHA `cf3d39c909004e954035648ceb9a2dd9854bd537182566de34eb4ebabeea5ca4`, review SHA `c181db87527a7509be605c3b505bc311d0995722edb34a1cfc487d196e06c93a`). R1013 adds exact active-selector mapping and per-chunk work/local-memory accounting rather than reopening those candidates. R980/R983 and the existing component/model receipts already bound the subgroup comparison; R1013 properly treats them as contrary/limited evidence, not a new win. Preserve the distinction among selector configuration, actual binary route, kernel timing and whole-model timing. No source-level change or performance conclusion follows from this report.

Retrieve only after a new source/build receipt identifies the active selector and a current same-path profile makes QSA ownership, matrix lowering, SLM, softmax or barriers a concrete decision. Require component and whole-dependency timing plus numerical, malformed-ID, padded-head, fault and 262,144-context gates before implementation adoption. The cited Intel/FlashAttention materials are design references; they do not qualify B570 execution.

**R1014 — accept with corrections.** It closes a real reachability gap: the generic plan allows 81,920 rows, but the pinned mode-8 admission, `run_moe_accum_window`, and `MoeWindowRoutes::build_accum` each cap at 65,536. It also correctly keeps the 8,192-row operator/job bound and identifies the whole callback/host-tail/lease path. The report is a source audit; it does not prove actual region fit, GPU free bytes, runtime allocation, end-to-end speed or numerical adoption.

Apply these corrections before relying on its checklist:

1. The spec's purported full-81,920 mixed allocation, 401,674,240 bytes, is the exact unaligned allocation for **78,452** rows. The report itself corrects this: full 81,920 requires `81920*2560*2 = 419,430,400` bytes (400 MiB); 78,453 rows require 401,679,360 bytes. The 78,452 batched versus 78,453 actual count must be reconciled with the real GPU-resident callback range before claiming one window.
2. Do not round a selected maximum down to a complete 8,192-row multiple when the goal is one window for a partial final piece. At 78,452 rows, a 73,728 maximum still splits the rows; the window maximum must cover the actual device-resident count. Distinguish the maximum row cap from the actual `rows` passed to the plan and from the size reserved for the mixed bank.
3. The region planner does not use a flat per-row coefficient. For each of its 17 planes it reserves `round_up(payload,256)+256`. Recomputing directly from the pinned formula gives 1,022,996,480 bytes at 78,452 rows and 1,023,006,720 bytes at 78,453 rows; the full 81,920 plan is 1,059,131,904 bytes. These arithmetic values still require comparison with actual `m.region_bytes` and do not establish physical fit.
4. Fallback language needs the R1012 correction (review SHA `f36ded666245ae7c245c4694474e46009248301979bdf69313ecf04d07c6c2be`): current mode-2 fallback occurs when VRAM/headroom admission does not admit mode 8. A `MoeAccumPlan`/shared-region failure or subsequent mixed allocation failure returns an error; it does not retry mode 2. Any changed adaptive-cap policy must specify and validate its own behavior rather than imply the existing fallback covers every failure.
5. Paying the full 400 MiB is a fixed-reservation choice, not inherent to an 81,920 maximum. The current admission sizes `count=min(cap,gpu_rows)` and its mixed allocation from that count. Document whether a new implementation reserves the full cap or only the admitted device rows.
