## Owner semantics and exact count boundary

Alloc::take reserves round_up_256(payload)+256 bytes: it rounds the typed payload up to 256, then adds one 256-byte guard. Every listed payload at these three chunk sizes is already 256-byte aligned. The eight inner GDN takes therefore add 2,048 bytes at every chunk size. Its returned size is an internal suballocation requirement; the outer take of the shared region adds another 256 bytes.

With --no-prefill-borrow, top-level Alloc takes call cudaMalloc separately and record their pointers as owned. The shared region is one such allocation; its GDN, QSA and MoE views are nested ranges inside that allocation, not three owners. By contrast, a borrowed cache-slot path carves all top-level takes from its supplied parent and uses Prefill::bytes_needed as a capacity bound. Those are distinct physical ownership cases. bytes_needed adds 8 MiB alignment slack to its count; init does not allocate that slack. init also has a separate unguarded tok_dev allocation of 4*T bytes, absent from bytes_needed. KV-stream mode adds conditional staging and a separately allocated identity table; whether either applies is not specified here. Layer-major hidden storage/cache and optional packed-QSA-prefix storage are further owners outside bytes_needed.

For the current alias=1 prefix planner, native16-accepted defaults (no unfused HC or BF16x2), the ten separate takes are emb FP32 T*N, R FP32 T*D, grs FP32 T*HC, xn16 BF16 T*D, lo FP32 T*LR, lo16 BF16 T*LR, gated FP32 T*D, inj FP32 T*HC, mixed_bf BF16 T*N and mixed_h FP16 T*N. Their aggregate guarded reservation is 124,832*T + 2,560 bytes: 511,314,432 / 1,022,626,304 / 2,045,250,048 bytes at 4,096 / 8,192 / 16,384. The mixed and bo FP32 takes are omitted because they alias emb; ple_emb also aliases emb under scratch_alias=1. The alias-OFF count path has the already recorded unused FP32 xn extent of 335,544,576 bytes at 8,192; it is not taken by the current alias=1 planner. The prior scratch-alias work and SYCL GEMM-workspace removal are existing baseline changes, not new proposals here.

## Typed views, liveness and required bridges

The current carve already uses temporal typed views inside short-layout FP32 reservations; these are not newly allocated native16 buffers. R16 and gated_b16 are disjoint halves of the old gated FP32 reservation and together consume the same T*D*4 bytes. QSA raw-K and normalized/rotated-K BF16 spans coexist in Kc and exactly fill its old FP32 extent; V16, Qfull16, q16 and attn16 use typed views over their FP32 owners. In MoE, GU_b16, Dm_b16, sgate_b16, sup_b16 and shared_b16 occupy half-width views over FP32 reservations. Xs/Hh, xn16, lo16, mixed_bf and mixed_h are already explicit 16-bit takes. Since the short route still requires its FP32 layout, reducing a type view alone cannot shrink those owners. At 4,096 QSA is the parent maximum; at 8,192 and 16,384 GDN is.

Prefix/bridge lifetimes are separate constraints. Token embedding uses emb, then PLE writes through ple_emb after layer-0 bo has been consumed; layer 1 reads PLE before its first mixed write. The current alias=1 layout deliberately gives these views emb's same T*N FP32 owner. Intermediate layer-major residual chunks are BF16 in the separate 10,240-wide residual plan; at 262,144 rows that logical bank is 5,368,709,120 bytes (5 GiB), independent of chunk size, with GPU/host split determined by runtime headroom. The final-layer callback restores BF16 into the existing FP32 R buffer, drains, then calls the decode/MTP/NLL float consumer. Keep R and its short-path use live across that bridge. If QSA math selects one of the packed-prefix modes, layer_major separately allocates two BF16 tensors plus flags: at a complete 262,144-cell prefix the source formula is 538,968,064 bytes. That request lease is neither the shared scratch region nor the residual bank.

Persistent state must not be folded into an arena reduction: GDN recurrence and convolution history are FP32 session state; QSA indexer tail/pools are FP32 session state, and QSA KV/RoPE owners remain session allocations with their physical page capacities. The native16 GDN kernel keeps local recurrence math/state FP32 even when it writes y16. Preserve layer order and causal chunk order, per-layer queue drains, cursor completeness, final FP32 callback, and lease restoration. The layer-major RAII lease drains on cancellation/early return/exception and aborts with temporary owners live if completion cannot be proven; field/callback restoration follows, while session tensors are not rolled back.

## Dedicated arena versus remapping; current 16K gates

A second dedicated long-only arena while the short owners remain allocated adds owners at peak and has no current source-based saving. Replacing current views in place is already how native16 is wired, but their containing reservations are still allocated at short FP32 extent. A truly smaller owner would require a full re-carve/high-water calculation that keeps short-layout buffers available and computes the max of all simultaneously live typed spans; a smaller view or fewer accounted payload bytes alone does not release an allocation. Generic borrowed cache-slot arenas have separate admission, relayout, and parent-capacity rules.

Current actual 16,384 blockers include layer_major.inc's m.T and m.T_max <= 8,192 precondition, and src/prefill/sycl/qsa_native16.cpp:25-31, whose shape() rejects tokens > 8,192 and is called by typed QSA norm/RoPE, gate, and append consumers. The routed-FP16 observer in prefill.cpp:1748-1751 has a separate 8,192 capacity guard but is optional diagnostics, not the native16 execution gate. Optional LayerMajorQsaProfile, boundary-audit and phase-audit helpers also cap queries/chunk at 8,192; they are not proof of a universal kernel limit. The GDN production wrapper has int32/size checks and no 8,192 ceiling, while the verifier has its own 8-token window. 16K remains unsupported here; no fit, speed or adoption claim follows from scaling arithmetic.

Bounded next Main decision: do not make a Dm-only or parallel-arena change as a capacity optimization. For C8192, an arena candidate must lower the complete GDN set by at least 59,718,912 B, the GDN-to-QSA gap, before it can shrink the shared region owner; otherwise the maximum remains GDN. Recompute every nested take guard and the outer region guard before selecting a source proposal. Unresolved for any exact direct-allocation total: loaded pack MAXBLOB, current session max_cells/KV mode, optional QSA prefix mode, runtime residual GPU rows, and separate layer-major allocations. Physical 262,144-context, RESTORE, numerical quality, fault, and performance gates remain independent and unqualified.
