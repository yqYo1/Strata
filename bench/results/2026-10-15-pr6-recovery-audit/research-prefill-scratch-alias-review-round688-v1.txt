Independent source review: prefill scratch alias v1
Date: 2026-10-15 (Asia/Tokyo)
Scope: read-only review of prefill-scratch-alias-v1/source.diff and source-review.md against the current source. No build, test, GPU run, cleanup, or existing-file edit was performed.

Conclusion

I found no concrete source-level aliasing or accounting defect in the proposed four-way FP32 alias (`emb`, `mixed`, `bo`, `ple_emb`) for the admitted single-GPU, full-model, explicit in-order SYCL queue topology. The proposal is plausible enough to proceed to production-path build and lifecycle validation, but it remains unbuilt and untested. This is source confidence only; it is not numerical, RESTORE/reuse, cancellation/fault, performance, admission, or physical-262144-context qualification.

Lifetime and phase review

The embedding write has one final pre-layer consumer: `gr_broadcast(m.emb, m.R, ...)` at current `src/prefill/prefill.cpp:1752`. After that, the FP32 slot can be reused. At each layer, `gr_mix`/`gr_mix_r` writes `mixed`; its FP32 consumers are the fused/MOЕ activation quantizers at current lines 2372-2373 and 2520. Attention projections consume `mixed_h`/`mixed_bf`, not the FP32 slot. The attention output `bo` stores at line 2307 occur after the attention half's FP32 mix consumers. The second half writes a fresh mix before its FFN FP32 quantization, and the FFN `moe_combine(..., bo, ...)` at line 2975 occurs after those quantizers. The residual write consumes `bo` at lines 3017/3021 before the next phase overwrites the shared address. For the oneMKL paths, the projection inputs are the separate half/BF16 images (`mixed_h`, `mixed_bf`, or `mixed_bf_lo`) and the projections' outputs reside in separate region buffers; the proposed FP32 alias therefore does not make a oneMKL input overlap its output.

PLE uploads its device rows at current line 1770, and the enabled predicate is confined to layer 1 (`ple_on` at 1630). Chunk-major calls reach `ple_land` after layer 0's final `bo` has been consumed (line 3032), then layer 1 consumes PLE rows at lines 2013/2039 before its first `gr_mix` at 2062-2064. Batched and token routes both read the same slot in this interval. In the long layer-major route, `run_layer_major` invokes `run_impl` one layer at a time (`src/prefill/layer_major.inc:336-383`); the layer-1 pass lands and consumes PLE before its mix, while layer 0 has no PLE consumer. This also covers partial final chunks because each pass uses its own `T` rows and queue order.

The draft/MTP callback path uses residual `R` and separately carved temporary buffers; it does not use these aliases. Queue work and host-to-device PLE copies are enqueued on `m.cs`; in SYCL the existing init binds `m.copy = m.cs`. Thus the source argument relies on queue ordering already present in the current SYCL route, not on a new synchronization assumption. Existing cleanup/owner behavior remains in place, but cancellation and asynchronous-fault lifetime behavior are unverified at runtime.

Planner and flag review

The proposal's `take_scratch_prefix` is shared by actual carve and alias-mode count-only sizing (diff lines 46-60, 106-112, 163-170). It omits the same two FP32 N-wide takes in both; the separate PLE take is omitted in both carve and sizing (diff lines 119-123, 174-180). The helper also preserves the conditional `xn`, BF16x2, and low-image takes, so these remain in the same order. The OFF branch retains the previous explicit count sequence and previous owned carve. Therefore default-off allocation layout and counts are preserved by inspection. Borrowed carve and owned carve use the same planner; the avoided take guards are accounted through `Alloc::take` rather than guessed separately.

The static mode accepts only absent or exact `0` as OFF and exact `1` in a SYCL build; all other values, and `1` in non-SYCL builds, return invalid mode (diff lines 28-37). Init checks that before allocations, requires a non-null stream and validates its in-order property through `queue_for` (lines 69-85; `include/strata/sycl/launch.hpp:47-57`). Peer-portable, attached peer, split ranges, handoff, and later topology changes are rejected in init/relayout/run, with `set_peer` rejecting a later attachment (diff lines 74-85, 130-145, 183-191). `bytes_needed` cannot inspect every instance topology, but it rejects peer-portable mode and init rejects remaining unsupported topology before carving; I found no successful smaller-layout fallback on those rejected paths.

For the requested geometry, `N=2560`, `HC=4`, hence `D=HC*N=10240`. At `T=8192`, one N-wide FP32 take is `8192*2560*4 = 83,886,080 B`. Three removed guarded takes save `3*(83,886,080+256) = 251,659,008 B` (240 MiB payload plus 768 B of Alloc guards), subject to the exact allocator alignment behavior. This is planned carve-span reduction, not measured free VRAM or throughput. It is additive to Main's already-present `GEMM_WS=0` patch; no workspace savings are attributed to this proposal.

I did not use the R686 extra 81,920-byte next-norm claim. The reviewed evidence says half 0's same-layer FFN norm and half 1's next-layer attention norm are distinct; the applicable lookahead is 40,960 bytes. That issue does not establish an alias conflict for the four N-wide buffers here.

Validation still required

The source review cannot establish actual owned/borrowed capacity and canaries, branch-specific SYCL compilation, correctness on short chunk-major and long layer-major routes, partial-tail handling, PLE batch/token/off behavior, or callback interactions. Before adoption, validate production `bytes_needed` against owned/borrowed carve and relayout, exact flag parsing, invalid stream/topology rejection, representative numerical outputs/logprobs/residual checkpoints, repeated request and MTP/graph restore behavior, cancellation and injected queue-fault cleanup, and full physical 262144 context correctness. Then take clean repeated long-prefill measurements. No 600/1000 tok/s, numerical, RESTORE, fault, or full-context claim follows from this source-only review.
