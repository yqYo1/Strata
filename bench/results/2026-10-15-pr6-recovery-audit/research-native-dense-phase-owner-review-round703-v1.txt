## Conclusion

I found no concrete owner-lifetime or error-restore defect in the candidate under its stated serialized, exclusive-reader contract. The critical transitions are ordered coherently: startup reserves CPU snapshots before publishing; bank entry allocates before withdrawing rows/freeing full owners; each layer prepares its host overlay before retiring the prior view, synchronizes the supplied stream before reusing bank bytes and publishes only after the H2D completion; full restore allocates and uploads every full owner before replacing table rows, then clears the overlay and frees the bank. Confirmed enter OOM/null returns with Full ownership/publication intact; the host policy explicitly classifies all other status/pointer combinations as uncertain. Restore OOM/null frees only the temporary partial full-owner set and leaves Bank state/table withdrawn for retry. Other checked runtime uncertainty paths fail-stop rather than returning an ordinary partial state.

The generator wrapper retires Verifier readers and all listed SessionGraphs families before withdrawing PLE/table publications. It restores a full NativeDense owner and separately rebinds the PLE key before session graph recapture and Verifier resume. The persistent `ss.ple` is not used as the mutated overlay: Prefill copies the PLE run/weights, injects the current layer-1 key into that local value before `ready()`, uses its weights for both PLE forms, and leaves history/state pointers on the original session. The `on_chunk`/MTP queue is synchronously drained at entry, activation, and restore. Layer-major's own RAII restores the borrowed overlay field and callback on normal return, false return, or exception; its checked pass/exception drains precede the outer weight restoration.

## Evidence and limits

- In the diff's NativeDense additions (`src/core/native_dense.cpp`, corresponding offsets around added lines 177–407), `load` makes packed copies and completes pending allocations plus vector/map reservations and layer-size accounting before the first `WeightRef` publication. `validate_table` checks every snapshotted row against its canonical/full or withdrawn value. Full restore keeps canonical metadata/data and native scratch identity, changing only the native payload owner address. The startup OOM classifier covers success/null, OOM/null, OOM/non-null, and non-OOM null/non-null combinations.
- In `src/program/generate.cpp` additions around lines 259–347, preconditions reject unsupported modes, preexisting hooks, token-graph handles, split graph capture and simultaneous head release; Verifier eligibility is checked before acquisition. Once readers retire, normal exceptions/false returns enter the same restoration lambda. If restore, recapture, or Verifier resume cannot be proven, the process stops before decode/RESTORE continuation.
- In `src/core/session.cpp` additions around lines 630–690, the new retire routine synchronizes the supplied replay stream, checked-destroys route/shared, exec/post, and split-prefix arrays, and resets capture pointers/flags. `Verifier::retire_prefill_dense` waits commit and drains compute/copy before destroying target-weight window/work graphs; its commit graph remains state-copy-only by the source comment and prior review. The candidate intentionally excludes split mode.
- `src/prefill/prefill.cpp` and `src/prefill/layer_major.inc` add overlay resolution only at the actual current and lookahead-norm view construction sites in the direct Prefill path. The PLE key's separate session publication is withdrawn before bank entry and restored from the full table after owner restore. `component_fixture.cpp` validates component payload/metadata and enter/restore flows in design, and the allocator shim arms only the first enter allocation; none of these fixtures has been executed, so they are test intent rather than evidence of passing behavior.

The contract remains caller-enforced: `ReaderRetirement{true}` is an assertion, not an API proof that every possible future reader joined the lease. This is acceptable only while the generator's request serialization, no-token-graph/no-split/no-window guards, explicit Verifier/SessionGraphs retirement, and synchronous callback assumptions remain true. The `SessionGraphs` destroy helper is designed for the selected configured SYCL, single-device route; this review did not establish safety for other backends or concurrent callers. Graph recapture failure deliberately aborts, and non-OOM/null/full-restore allocation failure and device fault behavior were not run. The component fixture hardcodes the existing 300-row no-native-PLE census; a native-PLE model fixture and complete generator lifecycle tests remain necessary as stated in the recipe. No conclusion is made about compiler/link correctness, graph capture behavior in a binary, state/output parity, RESTORE-and-continue, physical 262144 context, VRAM reclamation, or speed.

## Final finding

No new source defect to report within this owner/publication/restore scope. Retain the proposal's planned real-generator failure/cancel/callback and native-only-PLE qualification before integration; the source-level transaction is coherent, but its lifecycle contract is not yet experimentally qualified.
