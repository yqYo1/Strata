# R706 — Bounded quality qualification for inter-layer FP16/BF16 hidden storage

Date: 2026-10-15 JST. Read-only research and source audit. Written artifact only. No product edits, build, test, GPU/model run, Git mutation, process/environment inspection, artifact cleanup, private B access, or active-run inspection. This report does not qualify either storage format.

## Decision

The next useful experiment is a paired, same-token, fixed-placement three-arm screen on the existing natural 34,603-token thinking-template input: FP32 storage control, current FP16 storage, and BF16 storage once its separate source path is implemented and reviewed. Keep all live layer arithmetic and accumulations FP32. At each non-final layer boundary, do exactly one cast into the arm's storage format and restore once to FP32 at its next consumer. Keep the final callback FP32. Fix chunking, GPU-prefix/host-tail placement, model/cache/MTP configuration, runtime and source for all arms. Do not use the failing auto-24576 route as a control; retain its recorded FAIL.

This should be a screening gate, not a pass/fail claim from matching generated text or an invented universal loss percentage. First establish repeatability with two independent FP32 process runs, then compare candidate deltas against that observed baseline spread. If baseline variation is too large to resolve the candidate effect, improve the measurement before interpreting it. No public result found gives a defensible numeric FP16/BF16 acceptance margin for this exact quantized checkpoint and storage boundary.

## What the existing evidence already covers

The registry and R569/R573/R661/R662/R665/R666 reports cover the precision tradeoff, candidate source boundary, and proposed quality instruments. Avoid repeating these as new evidence:

- Current FP16 layer-major storage is implemented and has small-mechanics/build/conversion results, but no natural-history task-quality qualification. The synthetic 513-input path's final residual relative-L2 about 9.73% and one-distribution KL about 0.027 nats are fixture observations, not expected real-model quality loss.
- The post-hoc final-layer tensor screen (541 positions from 34,602 rows) measured one conversion's relative-L2: about 0.000211 for FP16 and 0.001677 for BF16. That screen skips the 47 repeated store/restore boundaries. BF16 remains a proposal/source-only storage arm, not an implemented or measured mode.
- The selected-logit natural-history screen sampled 19 correlated positions from one 34,603-token prompt. It observed median KL near 0.000994 nats and mean target-NLL delta near -0.0822, but the registry correctly says quality, full-context and RESTORE are unqualified. It was not a dense all-token NLL evaluation or an independent task sample.
- `prefill-selected-logits-v1` is integrated and its callback records up to 64 chosen full-vocabulary rows. The comparator reports agreement/error summaries; it is not an all-token teacher-forced loss evaluator.
- `prefill-teacherforced-nll-v1` is an unapplied source proposal. Its design computes stable FP32 target NLL for every prediction in the initial batched prompt and requires complete token/row coverage. It has not been built or run. R666 identifies external manifest binding and exact model/storage recipe as required before paired results can be interpreted.
- Existing baseline/long-path receipts establish FP32 layer-major behavior and exact-input pairing mechanics, not reduced-storage quality. They do not remove the need for a fresh same-binary paired comparison.

These facts are indexed in `research-index.json` under `residual_precision`, `selected_head_diagnostic`, and `pending_proposals`; detailed prior reports are named above. No need to add another logits callback: the current selected-logit callback and proposed NLL path already cover the respective roles.

## Minimal executable comparison

1. **Pin a common recipe.** Use the same exact 34,603 input token IDs and template, 34,602 batched predictions, native IQ3 checkpoint, source/binary/compiler/runtime/driver, chunk sizes and order, fixed GPU-prefix row count and host-tail, cache policy, MTP path and effective environment. Run separate owned processes, with interleaved arm order. Repeat FP32 twice. Reject incomplete callbacks, different IDs/configuration, nonzero device faults, non-finite results, or any candidate whose logged conversion differs from one cast and one restore per boundary. The external receipt must name the actual dtype and placement; the NLL proposal itself labels those as external recipe data.
2. **Measure all-token target loss.** Use the proposed `STRATA_PREFILL_NLL` initial-batched-prefill callback for every row `p=0..34601`, where `p` predicts the unchanged input token `p+1`. Compare paired mean NLL, per-position delta distribution, tails/max, and relative perplexity change; report aggregate and fixed subregions (early/middle/late, chunk edges, prefix/tail boundary). This adds only a few MiB of output (R666 estimates under 4 MiB per arm at the maximum 262,144-token input) and avoids storing full logits. It measures the exact long prefill history under teacher forcing, not free-running generations or a broad task population.
3. **Locate numeric drift without a full tensor dump.** At a small preregistered set of positions (e.g. 4: start, a middle chunk edge, prefix/tail edge, and late row), compare full-vocabulary logits by streaming one row at a time and report KL(FP32||candidate), target NLL delta, top-two margin and any top-1 flip. Add sparse residual checkpoints at a few layer boundaries (e.g. 1, 12, 24, 36, 47) for only those rows/branches. At each checkpoint compare restored candidate residual against the paired FP32 residual and record RMS/relative-L2, p99/max, non-finite/overflow/underflow-to-zero counts, broken out by layer/branch/placement. These sparse checkpoints answer whether error appears locally at one cast or grows over depth; they do not require a layer-by-layer full activation archive. Use a bounded small diagnostic buffer and remove it after verified summary. If the current path cannot expose a checkpoint without perturbing schedule/ownership, leave this submeasurement out and label drift localization unresolved rather than adding blocking per-layer readbacks to the main path.
4. **Check task impact cheaply, then expand only if promising.** Run a small, fixed paired screening set (about 12–24 distinct examples, not repeated seeds masquerading as independent examples) spanning retrieval, document QA, coding, reasoning/math and ordinary instruction; use fixed scoring rubrics and keep token sequence differences descriptive only. Record per-item outcomes and category; this is a screen to identify obvious failures, not a broad quality certification. For longer contexts first repeat the numerical/NLL screen at 64K if physically admitted, then run a bounded early/middle/late needle-retrieval screen there. Escalate to 262,144 only after the storage format survives shorter gates and the full-context run has actual memory/headroom admission. A 34K/64K pass never establishes 256K support.

The efficient first decision can stop after steps 1–3 if all candidate arms show a clear regression, conversion hazards, or effects below/above repeatability that need resolution; do not spend a broad task suite on an already unsuitable arm. If no obvious failure appears, keep the format as a candidate and proceed to held-out tasks and longer context. Before an adoption claim, use independent held-out prompts with paired confidence intervals, category/length reporting, and an agreed non-inferiority margin calibrated against FP32 repeatability. Prompt-token rows are correlated observations; they cannot be counted as thousands of independent task examples. The previous 299-independent-item / 1-point one-sided binomial rule and 0.5% relative-PPL bar are historical proposed thresholds (R569/R540), not validated or approved universal limits. Do not silently reuse them as quality facts.

## Source seams and resource bounds

The active route is rooted in `src/prefill/layer_major.inc`, planned by `include/strata/prefill/layer_major_plan.hpp`. The current FP16 boundary is around the `on_chunk` callback (layer-major line ~343 onward): FP16 packs the per-chunk residual with `to_f16`, writes the planned device/host span, and the next layer restores to FP32. The format planner accounts two bytes per stored scalar; BF16 would need a separately named dtype, conversion, plan test and explicit one-round-trip implementation before it can be included. Preserve FP32 as the live `m.R` and all operator/accumulation arithmetic. The final-layer `restore.chunk` forwarding remains FP32.

The final output seam is the existing `STRATA_PREFILL_LOGITS` callback in `src/program/generate.cpp` (selected rows only); the all-row NLL proposal extends that same final callback and reuses the native head path. It does not change model decisions. The proposed NLL artifact's maximum output is about 3.15 MB plus bounded metadata, below 4 MiB; selected logits should be streamed one row at a time, not retained for every position. Sparse residual snapshots for 5 boundaries × 4 rows × 10,240 values × 4 bytes are about 0.8 MiB per arm before modest metadata; keep only these samples and summary metrics, never complete residual histories or KV/session files.

At D=4×2,560=10,240 FP32 scalars/token, 34,602 rows consume about 1.42 GB decimal FP32 or 0.71 GB decimal 16-bit logical storage. For all 262,144 rows, this is 10 GiB versus 5 GiB, before placement/staging and other allocations. These are capacity arithmetic, not actual admission or measured savings. Existing planner must continue to account the 512 MiB headroom, expert cache, host staging, and actual free VRAM; do not infer full-context feasibility from the halved residual allocation. A physical 262,144 context claim requires a real admitted full context with decode headroom and the same correctness/quality checks.

Fail closed if the diagnostic has mismatched tokens/manifest, missing rows/checkpoints, invalid vocab/gold, incomplete output marker, NaN/Inf NLL or residual, overflow to infinity, unexpected conversion count, queue/runtime fault, changed arm geometry, or unknown resource retirement. Preserve each result's original status. Close/retire owners only after checked queue completion; if completion is uncertain, follow the existing fail-stop/retain-owner contract. Keep successful raw logits/NLL/sparse captures only until hashes and summary comparison are verified, then write a path/hash/reason deletion manifest per `docs/ARTIFACT_RETENTION.md`; do not clean during an active run or concurrent build/GPU work.

## Public primary evidence and limits

- Qwen Team's [Qwen3.8-Next architecture report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30320), also reviewed in R661, is the closest quality evidence: it discusses this model family's four-branch gated residual and reports FP8 inference residual-state storage at half the BF16 bytes with “almost no loss in quality.” The cited passage does not specify FP8 encoding/scaling, checkpoint quantization, tasks, context, numeric metric or the exact restore/arithmetic boundary. It supports testing the idea, not a Strata FP16/BF16 quality margin.
- Intel's [BF16 overview](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/developer/articles/technical/intel-deep-learning-boost-new-instruction-bfloat16.html) documents FP16 as 5 exponent / 10 fraction bits versus BF16 as 8 exponent / 7 fraction bits. This predicts FP16's finer significand and narrower range versus BF16's broader range and coarser rounding. It does not predict which repeated residual round-trip is better here. The CPU/GPU cast implementations must also be checked for matching rounding and special-value behavior.
- Kalamkar et al., [A Study of BFloat16 for Deep Learning Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.12322), report BF16 training results across language modeling and other tasks and motivate its FP32-like range. That is evidence for a different training workflow with mixed precision and trained models; it is not an inference residual-storage comparison. It cannot justify accepting BF16 layer boundaries on this already quantized IQ3 model.

No directly matched public FP16-vs-BF16 inter-layer residual-storage evaluation for this IQ3 checkpoint, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next long-prefill path, or Intel B570 was found. Current QSA pair maxima around 41.57 and all-finite samples describe a limited selected activation sample; they do not prove exponent headroom or precision quality across all layers/positions. Do not quote a universal loss rate. The paired experiment above is the bounded way to answer the actual question while keeping FP32 as the reference.
