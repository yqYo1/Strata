# Round 1035: QSA FP16-input/FP32-output fixture audit

Classification: decision support; immutable source-only snapshot review. I verified `manifest.json` before reading source: all five listed files match their recorded byte counts and SHA-256 values. The fixture and prototype remain uncompiled, unexecuted and unmeasured in this audit. I did not inspect live source or run a build/test.

## Finding

**Low severity — incomplete head-scratch sentinel coverage / overbroad `scratch_guards=PASS`.** In `fixture.cpp:144-145,154-158`, the FP32 head scratch is allocated as `nq * 4 * panel + 2 * guard` and initialized entirely to `score_canary`; the GEMM contract uses row stride `tile_blocks` (`qsa_score_gemm.hpp:7-12`, call at `qsa_score_gemm.cpp:67-70`). The fixture checks only the first/last 32 elements of the whole allocation (`fixture.cpp:305-315`). It never verifies per-row unused columns `[padded, panel)` remain canaries. The scorer should only write columns below `padded`; checking that suffix would catch an overrun from a row/column stride error. Therefore the final `scratch_guards=PASS` at `fixture.cpp:341` establishes outer guards, not all canaries in the head scratch. **Smallest correction:** after `heads.get()`, for each of `queries*4` rows, assert every element at `row*panel + col` for `col in [padded,panel)` remains `score_canary`; label the existing guard result `outer_scratch_guards` unless this is added. This is a coverage gap, not evidence that the current scorer overwrites the suffix.

## Reviewed gates

- **FP16-rounded-operand score oracle and prospective gamma bound:** `fixture.cpp:223-255` samples 67 block positions per query, computes each completed-block dot in double from operands first rounded through `sycl::half`, applies ReLU per head and accumulates four heads. `gamma128 * magnitude + gamma4 * (magnitude + abs(ref)) + 1e-7` at lines 242-252 is a defensible prospective FP32 summation-error envelope under the stated FP32 accumulation assumption; the FP16 operand conversion itself is included in the oracle, not miscounted as GEMM error. The separate dead-key path uses original FP32 inputs and is compared by exact bits against the baseline. This does not prove the installed oneMKL provider's accumulation/lowering or that unsampled blocks pass; the fixture appropriately labels the oracle prospective and reports its sample count.
- **Dead key and tail:** every query's dead-key output at `nb=n/4` is compared bit-for-bit with the FP32 baseline (`fixture.cpp:198-203`); the oracle separately includes the `n%4` bonus (`238-240`). This matches the prototype's explicit original-FP32 subgroup/tree dead-key path and tail bonus (`qsa_score_gemm.cpp:89-113`).
- **Independent selection and differences:** full-row expected selected cells are ranked independently from all `n` score cells, including weighted tail, with stable lower-ID ties (`fixture.cpp:90-104,257-262`). Original completed-score bit equality, max/mean absolute error, original-ID bit equality and selected-cell overlap min/mean are emitted (`193-220,327-333`). These are diagnostic differences, not an equality or task-quality gate, as the fixture comments/proposal say (`1-4`; `fixture-proposal.json:13-14`).
- **Other guards, packing, status, and lifetime:** score/ID outer guards and unused output tails are checked (`fixture.cpp:263-273`); inputs are compared bitwise after work (`300-304`); all active/padded pooled half words and query half words are checked (`317-326`); status is initialized, reset by the scorer, read after drain, required zero, and its outer guards checked (`141-165,188-190,276,286-293`). The fixture's `Drain` is declared after the `Gemm` and buffers (`136-148`), so its destructor drains before those owners are destroyed; the prototype requires those owners through checked completion (`qsa_score_gemm.hpp:14-21`). No concrete status-ordering or lifetime defect found.

## Limits

This source-only review does not prove compilation, API/provider selection, device execution, asynchronous library-internal ownership, actual FP32 accumulation, hardware lowering, performance, model-level score quality, or task quality. The prototype itself marks provider/lowering unmeasured (`qsa_score_gemm.hpp:18-23`); the fixture does not claim those results (`fixture-proposal.json:14-18`). No XMX/ISA qualification is required to accept the fixture's source-level sanity.
