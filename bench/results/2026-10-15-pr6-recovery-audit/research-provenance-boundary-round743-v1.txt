Historical reports and ordinary performance records that mention XeStrata policy are provenance documentation, not product implementation artifacts.

The cleanup record itself says it is not blanket license clearance. Existing R716 also leaves other unanchored candidates “unknown” by policy. This audit did not enumerate every historical Git object, every file outside the reviewed audit subtree, or every possible conceptual influence; therefore it cannot establish complete absence of any derivation. The focused current evidence supports no affirmative XeStrata-source finding for the selector scope or NLL proposal, while the NLL license-tag provenance and retained fixture tags remain the concrete unresolved metadata questions.
