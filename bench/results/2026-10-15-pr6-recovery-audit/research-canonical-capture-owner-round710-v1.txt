## Finding

The proposal is not compile-ready. A definite C++ source placement error puts executable startup logic inside `struct GpuStage`, at proposed `src/program/generate.cpp:1044-1056`. In the pinned base, `GpuStage` is a plain aggregate beginning at line 1000 and ending at 1018; it has no constructor or member function. The inserted `if` statements and `return 2` at proposed lines 1048-1055 are illegal at class scope, and refer to function-local names (`native_pack`, `o`) unavailable there. The patch also leaves the existing function-local `WeightTable wt` at proposed line 2397 while inserting another `wt` member in the struct, and captures the intended canonical snapshot as a struct member rather than as a local alongside the startup loader. This is a deterministic compile failure by source inspection; no compiler was run. Move the switch checks and `EngineSnapshot` declaration into the actual generator startup function immediately before the arena load, and leave `GpuStage` as an aggregate.

I found no additional definite source defect in the reviewed capture/materialization and owner-state paths. That is a bounded source conclusion only; it does not establish runtime safety or successful completion.

## Capture and ownership contract

The loader copies the exact host bytes used for each H2D upload into the snapshot at proposed `src/core/weights.cpp:456-464`, after the existing per-segment conversion has selected `stage_in` or `stage_out`. This covers copy, BF16-high, F32-to-F16, and FP16-scale widening paths at each chunk offset. It does not take a D2H snapshot or later reread source payloads. The snapshot is bounded by `capture_ram_limit` and exact arena size at lines 216-224; the proposal's 3 GiB payload cap is enforced before capture allocation. The capture fingerprint is FNV-1a, explicitly noncryptographic. Exact pack/file hashes and model identity are an external Main prerequisite, not established by this code.

`CanonicalDenseBank::adopt` verifies table/arena identity, row metadata, row extents, payload fingerprints, and disjoint extents before borrowing the generator's arena owner (`canonical_dense_bank.hpp:33-87`). It rejects NativeDense rows that still own overlapping canonical data and separately checks HC norms and persistent PLE rows. Prepare allocates bank and persistent pool while the old arena remains owned; entry republishes retained refs, withdraws other data refs, and frees the exact arena root (`:88-120`). Per-layer activation prepares the combined canonical/native overlay before clearing the old view and publishes only after its upload completes (`:121-132`). Restore retains the banks while uploading the full arena and publishing table refs, then leaves cleanup until PLE rebinding (`:133-150`). These source orderings look internally consistent under the stated exclusive reader lease.

The proposal corrects the earlier capacity error: canonical plus native original dense maximum is layer 1 at 142,024,576 B; the separate-max-expert plus dense case is 1,505,173,376 B. Its implementation keeps separate canonical/native banks and an 82,821,120-B persistent pool; it does not implement the 1,440,161,792-B adaptive combined expert/dense bank. The ledger's 3,821,010,432-B entry prepare overlap and 3,770,092,032-B sequential restore peak are target-weight payload arithmetic, not observed allocator headroom or physical recovery.

## Reader and behavior boundaries

The additional Verifier commit-graph retirement is warranted by the canonical norm pointers called out in the proposal and is placed inside the existing dense retirement lease (`src/core/verify.cpp:258-275`). The generator requests it before canonical release (`src/program/generate.cpp:351-355` in the source mirror). Native-only commit behavior is not changed. The combined overlay explicitly handles canonical versus native PLE key forms in the private Prefill `PleRun` (`src/prefill/prefill.cpp` near the `prefill_ple` composition); the PLE full/persistent fields are rebound before persistent storage is freed. The source also retains NativeEmbed as a required independent owner and rejects unsupported startup configurations.

Default-off behavior, reader exclusion, restoration, graph recapture, numerical parity, physical memory reclamation, cancellation/failure behavior in the real generator, RESTORE continuation, 262144-context correctness, and throughput remain unqualified. The policy/component fixtures and generator recipe are unexecuted, and the candidate has no compile result. In particular, the existing source-level retirement booleans and ordering do not prove that the configured runtime has released every graph reader or that `cudaFree` physically recovers memory.
