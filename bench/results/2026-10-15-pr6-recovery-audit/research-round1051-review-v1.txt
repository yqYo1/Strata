# R1051 review — R1050 exact K staging decision support

Recorded 2026-10-18. Classification: decision support; source/design only.

## Verdict and source audit

Accept for retrieval with the corrections below. R1050 (8,277 bytes; SHA-256 `b95c46ca911f1df870fe89f650b5483fccb451f6ab14c5f16054e3da1c1fe42d`) is pinned to the current `src/kernels/sycl/qsa_bf16_prefix.cpp` SHA-256 `fa505d02e2b24f7cb6948033921fa697c8aeb58e6f4df6d873f13e5a7918e36b`. The source resolves `qsa_bf16_prefix_optimized_batch` to `prefix_batch_impl<Cells+1,false,false>`: `Cells=32`, score stride 33, workgroup 128 and required subgroup 16. Its K producer computes `i=tid+128*n`, `cell=i%32`, `d=i/32`, reading `packed.k[rows[cell]*256+d]` or zero into `kv[d*32+cell]`. The K QK consumer loads from `kv+d*32+cell` with stride 32. V is subsequently staged in the complementary `[cell,d]` layout and consumed from `kv+c*256+dim_base+j*16`, stride 256. The K-only proposal correctly leaves those producer/consumer indices unchanged.

The proposed `cell=tid/4`, `q=tid%4`, `d=16*block+4*q+t` mapping is a bijection over the same 32x256 tile for `block=0..15`, `t=0..3`; each BF16 bit pattern can be copied unchanged to `kv[d*32+cell]`. Keep the `rows` sequence intact, including duplicates; preserve width/cap handling, tail and invalid-ID `-1` rows, live-cell/logical-page/table/physical-page checks, and zero fill. Also keep `packed.bad_rows`, `local_bad`/`local_live` aggregation and output status behavior intact. Every work-item must reach the existing row-publication and post-K barriers before QK. The subgroup-conditional matrix work remains uniform within SIMD16. The aligned `MatrixTile` base, QK stride 32 and PV stride 256 continue to meet the pinned matrix extension's 4-byte-base and stride-byte constraints; no probability, online-softmax, or FP32 arithmetic changes are implicated.

## Corrections and limits

- Treat transaction-sector reduction, vector-load generation, SLM bank conflicts, cache reuse, occupancy and speed as hypotheses. The 4-wide mapping changes both global lane addresses and SLM store ownership; only generated code and same-path measurements can establish the effect. The Intel memory and SLM guides give transferable design advice, not B570 transaction or bank mapping. In particular, do not claim the proposed 64-byte-separated local stores alias B570 banks without device evidence.
- `sycl_ext_oneapi_group_load_store` is experimental in the pinned Intel LLVM text. Its group-load iterator must be identical across members, and `full_group`, `contiguous_memory` and alignment properties are truthful assertions. It cannot directly load per-lane distinct row bases; a subgroup-per-row layout would need a common base and a separate transpose. Retain this only as a conditional alternative for the deployed compiler and bounds. The matrix API pin confirms the unchanged QK/PV layout contract.
- The current performance context is not an adoption result. The exact attention service binding reports 120 calls and 37.077867 seconds in the 78K prefill but expressly leaves cache/SLM/bank/occupancy saturation unresolved. Do not infer a K-stage bottleneck or speedup from it.
- The eligibility file names a closed serial cohort at 725.7 tok/s. Its cited `qsa-score-gemm-entry-model-comparison-v1.json` instead records medians 679.4 for `score_gemm=0` and 725.2 for `score_gemm=1`. Preserve the source labels and find the distinct receipt for 725.7 before using that number to establish future eligibility; this metadata mismatch does not change the source-only K analysis. The subgroup cohort is separate and remains Main-owned.
- R1044 already records C64 slower by 64.05% at 32K and 50.26% at 128K, C16 8.12% slower at 32K and only 1.50% faster at 128K, and the preserved 8192-query physical-262144 numerical failure. Neither C16/C64 nor the old packed candidate is reopened here. Keep the failure and all correctness/fault/context gates.

R1050's bounded comparison is suitable after Main's active cohort closes: compare only one K remap against the same `<33,false,false>` path, validate staged bits/output and invalid/tail cases first, then measure the full QSA dependency path through matrix use and completion. Preserve the V mapping, barriers, matrix calls, build/runtime and input order. A component result can select whether a whole-model paired comparison is useful; it cannot by itself qualify model speed, physical-262144 behavior, RESTORE, or adoption.
