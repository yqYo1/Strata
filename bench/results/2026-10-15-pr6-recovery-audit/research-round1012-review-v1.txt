Main's build and ten primitive results are provided context, not independently verified here; no full-model, physical-262144, RESTORE, numerical-quality, runtime-dispatch, or adoption claim follows.

The plan's generic `maximum_rows=81920` (`moe_accum_plan.hpp:16-18,41-44`) does not make 81,920 rows reachable in this mode. Mode 2 retains the 16,384-row route-factory/context cap; mode 0 remains the default (`prefill.cpp:441-461`).

For the admitted window, the checked arena plan reserves `rows*4` bytes for shared gates; the 65,536-row case is 262,144 bytes. The separately allocated mixed FP16 slab, shared `m.region` arena, persistent residual bank and `m.emb` BO chunk have distinct extents; the helper validates each device span and checks writable/input overlap (`moe_accum_plan.hpp:59-79`; `moe_window.inc:20-56`; `launch.hpp:15-43`). `min(T, rows-at)` covers partial last chunks in preparation, shared epilogue and HC writes (`moe_window.inc:77-91,143-151`).
