The producer activates capture only when the chunk intersects `[98304,98336)` and captures rows in that bounded slice for layer ordinals 0 through 12 (`prefill.cpp:3540-3559`). It emits `R_input` before the two layer halves (`:3655`); on QSA layers 3, 7 and 11 it then captures K/V quantization inputs and raw indexer rows, normed/rotated Q and indexer Q, one score row per physical row after score computation, per-row step and selected-ID frames after top-k, and attention output after attention (`:3872-3892,3927-3947,4117`). Each half ends with the corresponding residual capture (`:5220`). This gives exactly three residual frames for each of 13 layers, plus 102 additional frames on each of three QSA layers: 345 records per full GEN, in the order `expected_full_records()` encodes.

Shape arithmetic also matches the source: hidden/residual width 10240, QSA K/V rows 512, indexer raw 128, Q width 6144, Q-indexer width 512, attention output width 6144; 32 captured rows. Score-frame width is `step.n_bid + 1 = floor((position+1)/4)+1`, and selection width is `min(position+1, idx_top_k + idx_block - 1)`, 2051 for these positions. Each step frame is four int32 values: position, `n_kv`, completed blocks, and selection width. With 345 frames, source geometry is 66,747,936 bytes per full GEN and 133,495,872 for the pair, 721,856 bytes below the 128 MiB cap. The v23 root correction remains authoritative: cell 98328 is the start of pooled block 24582, the block completes at 98331; QSA ordinal 2 maps to model layer 11. The unchanged C output evidence was four generated token IDs, not internal selection IDs.

`owned_full` requires the exact five GEN names/order, contiguous half-open intervals covering the file, empty intervals for the three non-full requests, and two nonempty intervals for the two fresh full requests. Each entire record must fit inside exactly one full interval. It validates 345 ordered unique semantic records separately per request; ordinal is process-global from zero across both full reads. The pair comparator correctly matches semantic identities while ignoring the changing global ordinal, compares hashes, rereads differing payloads in bounded chunks, and reports the first raw-word difference in left-manifest hook order with row, column, byte offsets and raw/value summaries. This means “earliest” is accurately limited to the first captured hook that differs; it cannot identify an earlier uncaptured operation.

Numerical anomalies remain data rather than parse errors: complete records retain NaN/Inf/step/ID findings, JSON avoids NaN literals, and a later framing error returns a partial manifest preserving completed earlier records/findings. A structurally valid numeric finding does not set `original_C_rejection_cleared`, adoption, performance or lifecycle flags. This preserves the key distinction between malformed capture and numerically suspicious capture.
