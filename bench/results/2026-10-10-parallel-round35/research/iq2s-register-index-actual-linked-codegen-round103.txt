Code-generation comparison
The archived R99 candidate had a 0xb8-byte local stack allocation and a fully unrolled inner body. It repeatedly wrote packed 16-bit index vectors to `[rsp]`/`[rsp+0x10]` and reloaded their lanes with `movzwl`; it also had additional YMM stack traffic. R99 correctly held that version before timing.

In the new linked candidate, the local allocation is 0x20 (32) bytes; the direct control allocates 0x28 (40). Candidate stores a 32-byte sign buffer once at `[rsp]` and later reads its sign words through that buffer. This is the intentional `memcpy` scratch present in the control as well. Within the candidate's hot body there are no packed-index stores/reloads or other repeated stack round trips. The accumulators stay in YMM registers through the inner loop; I see no additional accumulator YMM spill. The smaller subtraction is a local-area comparison, not total frame size: the two functions also save different sets of callee-saved GPRs.

The candidate has the same two-level loop shape as control: the outer block loop and a four-iteration inner loop stepping by two 32-value groups (eight groups for each 256-value block). That restores compact looping versus R99's fully unrolled candidate. Per inner iteration, candidate obtains two `high[qh]` 64-bit table values, interleaves each with eight low q bytes, extracts the eight used 16-bit indices with eight `vpextrw` instructions, and performs the same eight scalar IQ2_S grid-table reads. Control forms the same index values with per-byte loads and qh shifts/masks/ORs, then performs those same eight scalar grid-table reads. The 256-entry `high` map is a 2 KiB read-only lookup dependency; this is a source-level/mechanism trade, not proof of cache behavior or speed.

After index generation, the decoded-grid vector construction, sign transform, `vpmaddubsw`, `vpmaddwd`, integer accumulation, per-block scale multiplication/FMA, and final horizontal reduction remain present in the captured sequence. Candidate and control use different registers and instruction addresses, but the ordered dot/reduction path is visibly retained. The direct-control control's inner loop likewise processes four iterations and accumulates two YMM integer vectors before the unchanged block scaling and final reduction. Source review R101 confirms the source delta kept the arithmetic and ordered updates fixed.

Disposition
The specific R99 codegen failure is resolved in the exact linked candidate: index-array stack materialization/reloads are absent, no replacement accumulator spill is visible, local stack use returns to the sign-copy scratch scale, and the inner loop is compact again. This passes the static linked-codegen gate and supports proceeding to the separately authorized actual-weight timing comparison. It does not establish that candidate is faster, and it does not justify adoption; timing and representative-workload results remain outstanding.
