## How to read the pair without adding hooks

Compare `full256k-first` with `full256k-repeat` using the reader's `compare_requests` on their parsed `owned_full` manifests. Use its `first_difference` semantic identity and the full `different_records` count, preserving the fixed-window and original-rejection caveats.

- If layer 9 `R_input` is the first differing record, the residual was already different at layer 9 entry. Check the immediately preceding `R_post_moe` at layer 8 in the same pair: if it differs first, the earliest captured difference is before/within layer 8's FFN residual path; if layer 8 post-MoE matches but layer 9 input differs, inspect the boundary between those adjacent layer calls.
- If layer 9 `R_input` matches but `R_post_attention_gdn` is first different, the captured divergence is within layer 9's GDN half, its GDN output projection, or its residual/control write. This capture has no internal layer-9 `qkv`, conv output, recurrent output, or GDN state records, so it narrows the responsible half but cannot distinguish those operations.
- If the layer-9 post-GDN residual matches and `R_post_moe` first differs, localize the captured change to layer 9's MoE half or its residual/control write.
- If all three layer-9 records match, continue in reader order through layer 10's three boundaries and then the layer-11 QSA records. A first difference in layer-11 `K_quant_input` or `indexer_raw` after matching layer-11 `R_input` places the earliest captured mismatch inside that layer's input-normalization/projection path rather than proving a layer-9 defect.

This inference is about the earliest difference among the captured payloads, not necessarily the earliest internal arithmetic difference. `R_post_moe` of one layer and `R_input` of the next are separate records of adjacent hooks; their pairwise agreement is a useful boundary cross-check.

## Limits

This capture only covers 32 rows in the chunk intersecting `[98304,98336)`. It does not capture the recurrent GDN state, GDN intermediate tensors, or other token rows/chunks. Thus it can identify the first changed *record* in this bounded window and decide whether new layer-9 internal hooks are justified, but equality across the capture cannot clear the original full-run math rejection or exclude a difference elsewhere. If the first difference is layer-9 `R_post_attention_gdn` with equal input, only then consider a narrow follow-up at the existing GDN block around `prefill.cpp:3725-3740`; do not add it speculatively before reading the pair. The capture reader explicitly keeps `original_C_rejection_cleared=false`, `adopted=false`, `performance_eligible=false`, and `full_lifecycle_passed=false`.
