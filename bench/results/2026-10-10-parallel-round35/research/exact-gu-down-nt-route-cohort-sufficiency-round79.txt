## Counting rule

For each `CACHE_ROUTE_PAIRS_V1 PAIR layer expert entries hits refused offloaded callback_pairs` row, let `n=entries`, `j=callback_pairs`. An ID is eligible for an NT cell only if `refused>0` and its logical job count is positive: `J1=2*j-n` for NT1, `J2=n-j` for NT2. Group the ID by that layer's exact `(GU type, Down type)` from the metadata. Deduplicate `(layer,expert)` within a request and across the duplicate A/A or B/B captures. Report A and B as separate presence sets, and their union/intersection. Logical callback-job volume and repeated requests do not add unique IDs. This follows the established R42/R63 pair-to-NT rule; no parser or replay code was run.
