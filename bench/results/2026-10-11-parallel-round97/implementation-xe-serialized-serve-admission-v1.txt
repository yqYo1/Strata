Cause and smallest scoped repair
The old local graph_hits variable meant cache-hit eligibility (hit_fn, loaded
profile, CPU pool enabled), not that a graph had actually been captured. Its
three consumers were its definition, residency setup, and the serve admission
condition. The setup condition additionally required host_handoff_concurrent,
which is universally false for the current unqualified volatile protocol.
Therefore the real selected serve invocation had graph_hits=true but no
host_res/d_res, and returned 2. Merely deleting that admission test would fail
at Verifier::init instead: verify.cpp requires d_res, cache_base and blob for
the profile-filled VRAM expert tier.

Only generate.cpp changes (8 inserted lines, 6 removed):
1. Rename the three local references to cache_hits, reflecting their meaning.
2. Prepare the existing residency table when cache_hits and o.serve, independently
   of concurrent handoff/token-graph flags. For non-serve invocations, preserve
   the exact old concurrent-handoff/capture/dump predicate. The preparation body,
   allocations, checked synchronous copies, stage tables, arena release/prefetch,
   slot mappings and TokenHits bindings are unchanged.
3. Replace the misleading "token graph hit path ... decided on the device" setup
   log with "expert residency table: ... resident experts". No whole-token graph
   success is claimed by metadata initialization.
4. Preserve the spec/prefill/table admission predicates and exit status; rename
   the cache diagnostic to the verifier hit path rather than the graphed hit path.

The o.serve exception is deliberate. Making this setup unconditional for every
cache_hits invocation would also assign drive.d.host_res in the ordinary
one-token session_loop fallback. expert_pool_dispatch has a separate, unchanged
local graph_hits meaning static host_res membership; expert_hit_run can dynamically
admit experts in that ordinary loop. A startup static table is not a substitute
for its per-layer d.is_hit decision. The scoped repair leaves those non-serve
conditions unchanged and avoids that extra behavior change.
