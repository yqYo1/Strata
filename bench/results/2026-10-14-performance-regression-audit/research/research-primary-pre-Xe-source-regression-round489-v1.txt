## Finding

The history does contain substantial user-authored SYCL inference work between Oct 4 and Oct 10, beyond the Oct 4 tune-up covered by R466. It is preserved in commits reachable from the archived source merge `4526800257fe49c0db02a414f21b38dac2586e54`, but that merge's first parent is the `feature/sycl` source snapshot `f4c2b8dd` → `4fa478f50f8da396e651e3ae862f8b1849d49b54`; its other parents are parallel histories, not integrated features. `4fa478f` itself is the Oct 9 retention-doc commit over `22e61b95`; its tree is the actual current primary source under investigation. Therefore ancestry preservation at `45268002` must not be read as adoption.

A source check also rules out a blanket claim that all accepted Oct 4 positives vanished: 4fa has the direct prompt output, compact exact XMX path, prompt ring/same-queue controls, native IQ ESIMD dispatch, CPU `STRATA_IQ_SINGLE_EXACT`, and native expert-hit graph. R466's measured Oct 4 positives remain selectable in current source. They may be disabled by a launch override or inapplicable to int8/resident-KV settings, but that is configuration/routing rather than missing implementation. R466 records that the `--native <GGUF>` path already activates the native preset; accepted 4K prefill ran FP16 KV/context 8192/cache 512, while today's long diagnostic uses int8 resident KV/context 262144/cache 128. These are unlike measurements, not proof of source regression.

The concrete source-level loss found in the otherwise prominent accepted positive is narrower: the old `e8a330cd` candidate captured an ordinary layer's host handoff *and next route* together. Current 4fa `src/core/session.cpp` explicitly rejects `post_inputs->route_next` unless `layer_shared_early()` (`session_capture: next-route posts require separate shared work`). Current 4fa has native expert-hit graph capture, but that is a different graph contract and does not establish next-route capture. The archived Oct 3 measurements report a 3.1% ordinary decode increase (18.93→19.51 tok/s) for captured native expert-hit graphs; this supports the graph feature but does not isolate the missing next-route posts. No retained evidence found in this audit quantifies a speed gain attributable specifically to `e8a330cd`'s combined handoff/next-route capture. Treat it as a measured-positive-path capability gap, not a demonstrated cause of the current 32K regression.

## Why R466 missed the larger trail; what the records establish

R466 is a sound reconciliation of Oct 4 settings, but its declared boundary is the 4fa first-parent tree and its record set is Oct 2–4. It did not enumerate all commits in the parallel faithful/SYCL history between Oct 4 and Oct 10. The `45268002` merge commit is a useful reachable archive index: parent list is `f4c2b8dd`, `28a7b9ae` (Xe), `8e85169a`, `1cd44298`, `4bc14f2c`, `c7d59d7b`, `9e57d6e6`; first parent `f4c2b8dd` is only the Oct 14 docs commit over 4fa. Thus the source ledger needs two axes: origin/source-history presence versus actual current resolver/call-path presence. Neither commit reachability nor a higher number on another configuration is inference adoption or a regression measurement.

The October 4 prompt measurements are genuine positives on their recorded workload. The Oct 4 whole-model worker/chunk measurements are also retained: 826-token prefill 46.898→14.083 s (chunk 64→1024, combined change), but context 2048/cache 1649 and no decode speed claim. Historical short-context decode medians are not comparable to today's 32K position. The main's current native run rates and the Oct 4 rates differ in input position, KV representation/residency, cache size and workload completion, so source history alone cannot explain the observed regression.

## Conclusion for regression triage

Current evidence does **not** support “Xe work removed all pre-Xe measured optimizations.” Major Oct 4 measured-positive routes are still in 4fa source and can be gated by launch configuration; several Oct 4 numbers are workload-specific. One separable old graph capability (captured next-route with ordinary handoff) is absent from 4fa source, but no measurement isolates its speed contribution. The Oct 5–10 commit set needs a per-component run.json/source hash mapping before any further positive-loss claim. This source audit identifies recovery candidates; it does not diagnose the present 32K performance regression.
