## Finding

The intended model remains clear: Niko1221/Strata is the sole upstream; `yqYo1/Strata` is the personal fork; `feature/sycl` is primary; XeStrata is an optional source of individual ideas. Prior R458's active-SYCL report establishes that fork main's default CMake path selects `sycl/` and returns before the CUDA/HIP root engine; the main-side SYCL overlay has behavior not present in P's committed tree. It specifically identifies dynamic VRAM resizing through SYCL virtual memory as a real source-level candidate/gap, while root CUDA BGEN/VRAM paths do not by themselves establish a required CUDA/HIP port. R461 did not re-run or revalidate those CMake/protocol conclusions. The R458 `research-index.json` is the supporting report of record; do not substitute a blanket engine import for per-feature evaluation.

The non-inference reconciliation obligation remains separate: requested Niko non-inference features need an inventory and disposition against P, with shared CLI/API/service behavior distinguished from GPU-backend-only capabilities. Current `main` being the fork default is still explicitly pending in the existing runtime and service-port records. A small next step is a source-only, component-level delta ledger for Niko main vs P that identifies each non-inference function, active SYCL source selected by CMake, and a disposition (`present`, `missing and applicable`, `backend-specific/not in scope`, or `deferred`); include dynamic VRAM as a candidate and verify its owner/API before deciding. Then reconcile only applicable items on primary and update default at an approved completed boundary. No evidence here warrants importing the entire CUDA/HIP engine or keeping a Xe checkout alive.
