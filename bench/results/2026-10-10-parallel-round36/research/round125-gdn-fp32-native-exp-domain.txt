## What source and flags establish

Intel's DPC++ guide describes `-fp-model=precise` as value-safe floating-point transformations and, in the general compiler option table, says precise sets `fp-contract=on`; `consistent` disables contraction. Thus precise is not a blanket prohibition on FMA contraction. It also does not prove that every source local is materialized to memory. In this exact producer, however, there is no direct multiply-plus-add expression in the gate assignment to contract: it is a conditional result multiplied by `ssm_a`. The earlier `v` addition also feeds the comparison and `exp` branch. The source/flag audit identifies no source-level `a*b+c` contraction in this expression, but only emitted IR/device code for the exact compiler can settle actual lowering and intermediates.

`float` types and the global `float` store establish the interface/storage width; they do not establish correctly rounded transcendental results. SYCL's `sycl::native::exp` specification explicitly says both its valid input range and maximum error are implementation-defined. OpenCL C likewise says `native_exp` operates over an implementation-defined range with implementation-defined maximum error, and says denormal support for `native_` functions is implementation-defined. `-fp-model=precise` and correctly-rounded divide/sqrt do not replace that contract. Ordinary OpenCL `exp` has defined accuracy requirements, but that is a different function and cannot be silently substituted as the native function's guarantee.

Consequently the source establishes a binary32 gate-buffer handoff but not (a) forced materialization of every source local, (b) a native-exp approximation error bound, (c) a valid-input interval for this Intel GPU compiler/runtime/driver, or (d) native-exp subnormal/FTZ behavior. No public version-specific Intel compiler/device declaration for the selected `native_exp` interval or denormal behavior was found in this review. Do not infer such a declaration from the fact that the build targets Intel GPU, from the generic `-fp-model=precise` flag, or from an observed result on an unspecified device.
