Strata artifact retention policy (2026-10-09)

Commit and push source, decisions, measured summaries, reproducible commands and failure/adoption reasons at natural boundaries. Resume work from committed reports; avoid separate progress-backup trees.
Keep a canonical set of concrete32K/full262144 numerical fixtures and the actual32K RESTORE input while current strict comparisons consume them. Retire old-version/candidate captures once outcome fingerprints and comparisons are recorded. A failure status alone does not justify retaining every prior tensor dump.
Default debug logging: validation plus errors/warnings, project chunk/phase progress and final outcome. Full all-API success tracing requires a specific unresolved allocation/lifetime hypothesis and a bounded window/size budget. Never use logged runs as speed comparisons.
After each experiment, retain compact immutable results/source/env/protocol and review detail files. Do not preserve every normal trace or every repeated failure. Use one representative full failure trace where whole history is needed; otherwise retain relevant final window, stack/driver dump and exact structured error. Verify lossless compressed original/decoded SHA256 and size before removing a needed raw trace.
Raw profiler event histories are disposable after their useful summaries are recorded. Do not store giant traces/tensors in Git. Record retired paths and reasons; keep original numerical and fault statuses unchanged.
All cleanup/builds/tests/GPU/profiling/compression are scheduled by root and serialized. Research reports remain read-only leads, repeated with shifted scope and the full shared report registry.
Home-wide ZFS snapshots are outside project-file cleanup; report their actual effect on available space separately.
