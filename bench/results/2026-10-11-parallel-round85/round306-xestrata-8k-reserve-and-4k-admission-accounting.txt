## Cache budget, physical capacity, and actual fill are distinct

The argv asks for `--expert-cache 128 --expert-cache-per-layer` and the profile has 24,576 ranked pairs. `generate.cpp:3571-3593` treats 128 as a byte budget of 128 times the largest 2.66 MB blob. It divides the cap by the sum of one 256-byte-aligned blob per each of 48 layers, obtains quota 3, and allocates 48 × 3 = 144 physical slots. The trace confirms `128 uniform slots (0.32 GiB) -> 144 slots, 3 per layer (0.27 GiB)`. `INFO expert_slots=144` reports physical capacity, not the number filled.

The profile-fill loop at `generate.cpp:3745-3758` visits the globally ranked list in order. With per-layer admission, `ExpertCache::admit` refuses a pair once that layer's three-slot range is full (`expert_cache.cpp:432-445`); the caller breaks on the first `kNotResident` at `generate.cpp:3747-3748`, rather than skipping that pair and continuing other layers. Thus the measured 29 prefilled / 144 physical is real and source-consistent. `verify_slot` verifies the first filled pair only (`:3760-3767`); `token graph hit path: 29 resident experts` agrees with that occupancy. Do not call this a 144-expert filled cache, infer 144 decode hits, or compare its requested byte budget with a physical slot count as though they were equal. Per-layer quota plus global profile order is a decode-coverage limitation in this exact arm, separate from the reserve guard.

## What 8K to 4K can release, conditionally

The run sets `--max-context 262144`, `--prefill 8192`, `--kv int8 --kv-resident 32768`, and `--no-prefill-borrow`; the prompt path therefore owns its chunk buffers instead of borrowing cache slots. The 3.09 GiB pinned host K/V reported in the receipt is host RAM and is not VRAM recovered by lowering prompt chunk. Max-context KV/session capacity, the 32K resident K/V tier, head, expert cache and draft binding likewise remain capacity allocations unless their options change.

`Prefill::bytes_needed` at `prefill.cpp:1000-1041` mirrors the `init` allocation order. It combines fixed GEMM scratch, T-scaled row/route/projection buffers, one shared region sized to `max(gdn_set_bytes(T), qsa_set_bytes(T,...), moe_set_bytes(T,...))`, constant dequant/group workspaces, the ring, PLE buffers, and the KV-stage buffers. For this receipt's requested ring 8, `ring_slots` clamps the effective value to 16 (`:149-164`); both 8192 and 4096 exceed `STREAM_ALL_MIN=2048`, so the stream-all threshold remains crossed and the ring branch remains the same 16 slots. The two sizes also both use the same stream-all MMQ group buffers when those layers are enabled (`:1025-1034`). Max-context-dependent selection/stage terms, the fixed 64 MiB GEMM scratch (32 Mi FP16 elements), group buffers and ring capacity do not halve with T. The chunk-scaled terms do.

The source's coarse planning estimate is `160 MiB + 680 KiB * chunk` (`generate.cpp:3473-3480`), which changes by 2720 MiB from 8192 to 4096. That estimate is explicitly used for cache planning and is not the exact source allocation receipt. The observed 3923-to-265 MiB phase is a net 3658 MiB after the prior marker, but the receipt does not give a finer allocation breakdown; it cannot be used as an exact prompt-scratch total. The native head weights themselves were loaded before the 4204 MiB marker. The code's exact source calculation is `Prefill::bytes_needed(8192)-Prefill::bytes_needed(4096)`: it must use the loaded geometry and `mmq_plan`, session shape, alignment, and actual effective ring. Both chunk-scaled terms and the `max(gdn,qsa,moe)` shared region change; fixed GEMM/group/ring/max-context pieces remain. The only standalone numeric forecast available from the inspected source is therefore the coarse 2720 MiB planning proxy, not a measured or exact allocator delta. Report it as approximate source planning only. Do not claim a 4K fit: startup admission, allocator rounding/fragmentation and subsequent verifier/draft allocations still decide it. A 4K run must keep the real reserve guard and report its own `INFO` free VRAM.

The receipt's own startup advisory says 50 MiB leaves the GPU LOW and suggests a reserve of 1162 MiB or lower context; the owner uses a separate 512 MiB minimum. A source estimate that crosses 512 is not evidence of the 1162 MiB reserve, and neither boundary should be weakened to manufacture a pass.
