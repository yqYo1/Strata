## Findings retained from R211

This correction does not change the source-level macro finding. In the exact `compile_commands.json` for `verify.cpp` and `prefill.cpp`, compilation uses `-DSTRATA_BACKEND="xe" -fsycl`, and there is no `-DSTRATA_ENABLE_SYCL`. The configured CMake option is `STRATA_ENABLE_XE=ON`; it is distinct from the absent source macro. Thus the `STRATA_ENABLE_SYCL` conditional branches are not selected for those compiled XE translation units. `STRATA_SYCL_PREFILL_SAME_QUEUE` does not force these queues together. `Prefill::init` receives its compute stream and creates a separate copy stream; Xe's runtime constructs in-order compute and transfer queues and creates new in-order streams. The wrapper maps synchronization and events to SYCL queue finish, waits and barrier dependencies. Some intermediate prefill copy/event return values remain unchecked, as R211 noted.

Likewise, the source analysis of `STRATA_VERIFY_SEGMENTED=1` remains: graph segments are synchronized before host-side sequence reads, CPU planning/publication precedes launch of the next segment, and non-SYCL-guarded wait kernels remain in this compile. This is a serialized ownership path; it does not establish concurrent host/device polling safety when segmentation is off.
